# AI-Fuel-Prediction                                                                     

*About Fuel Predictor Application:*

SHUDHI AI is a smart AI application that predicts your vehicle's fuel future.

It tells you how many kilometers you can travel with your current fuel, detects if your fuel is pure or adulterated, and finds the cheapest and purest petrol bunk near you.

Our app saves your money, protects your engine, and stops fuel fraud.

One app for complete fuel safety.
